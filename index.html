Paid Media and Bidding AI Agent

The paid media and bidding AI agent from Nestack Agent Care treats a bid change as the spending commitment it is. It records what changed, how far the change could move spend, who approved it and the standing authority it ran under.

Proposes the change, never approves the spend

When a bid ceiling is raised, the agent works out how far an automated strategy could carry it overnight before anyone reads the proposal. When a budget shifts between lines, the authority that permits it is named on the change. When a platform recommends more spend, the source of that recommendation is labelled, and the part of a change that cannot be undone afterwards is shown beforehand.

One change, proposal to approval

  • Change evidence arrives from bid rules, budget records, platform recommendations or delivery feeds.
  • The agent assembles the line, the budget behind it, the authority it runs under and the day it was raised.
  • It drafts a proposal with the change, its exposure, the authority needed and its completeness.
  • Authority checks, exposure ceilings and reversibility checks are applied before the decision gate.
  • Changes inside the standing limit go to the paid media lead; anything past it adds a finance manager read first.

What stays with people

Approving a change above the standing limit, setting the spending limits themselves, committing the brand to a platform contract and deciding what the media budget is for all stay outside the automation boundary. The agent writes to budget and bid records only where write access and records policy allow it, and every finance correction is counted in its evaluation.
